## Available commentary

Sarch can provide plain-English commentary for journalists, professional associations, advisers and community organisations on:

- the NDIS provider-registration pathway;
- registration groups and scope selection;
- verification and certification audit preparation;
- common documentation and evidence gaps among small providers;
- the operational difference between having a policy and evidencing that it is used;
- worker screening, qualifications and compliance records;
- registration preparation for support workers, cleaners, trades, allied health and SIL providers;
- the respective roles of the NDIA, the NDIS Quality and Safeguards Commission and approved independent quality auditors;
- startup costs and practical risks for new disability-service businesses.

- We do not claim that a consultant can approve, fast-track or guarantee registration.
- Registration decisions are made by the NDIS Quality and Safeguards Commission.
- Audit services are supplied independently by approved quality auditors engaged directly by the applicant.
- Statements about current requirements should be checked against official government and regulator sources before publication.
- We will distinguish direct evidence from opinion and will identify limitations in any original analysis.

## Public-data project status

DGTG Pty Ltd has developed an aggregate-only analysis pipeline for the public NDIS Commission Provider Register. The official CSV endpoint did not deliver data to the automated runner during repeated tests in August 2026, so no statistics have been published and no figures should be attributed to this project yet.

A separate request for existing aggregate registration records has been submitted through the Commission's information-access process. Any future report will disclose its source, methodology and limitations and will not publish provider names, ABNs, contact information or row-level records.
