## How this series is maintained

- Articles are written in Australian English for prospective and newly registering providers.
- Regulatory claims are supported by links to official NDIS, NDIS Commission or Victorian Government sources.
- Each article shows its publication and review date.
- Time-sensitive pages use exact calendar dates rather than vague references such as “soon”.
- Commercial involvement is disclosed clearly.
- The articles do not claim government affiliation, endorsement, guaranteed registration or influence over an audit or Commission decision.
- The website guides remain the primary service resources; these articles are original supporting explanations rather than copied mirrors.
